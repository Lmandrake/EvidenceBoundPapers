# Evidence audit

The checker proves a quoted span exists in the cited source. It cannot prove the source
supports the claim's **scope**. Fill this before each prose regeneration, one entry per
load-bearing claim. A claim may not enter the paper unless its source directly supports its
scope; a rescoped claim is re-admitted by a human at a G3-revision gate. A filled-in example
is `example-synthetic/04-log/evidence-audit.md`.

## Format

```
## SUP-nnnn: <short name>
- exact claim: <as currently written>
- type: empirical | causal | comparative | gap magnitude | ...
- scope as written: <population, period, conditions the wording covers>
- source and exact passage: <key>: "<passage>", plus any qualifying sentence nearby
- support level: direct | partial | adjacent
- counter-evidence: <in the corpus, including the source's own caveats>
- revised language: <or "unchanged">
- confidence: high | medium | low, and why
```
