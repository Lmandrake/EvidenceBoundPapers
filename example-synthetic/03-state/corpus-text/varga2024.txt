Varga, A., Halvorsen, T. and Ibarra, L. (2024). Residual drift in deep profiling floats.
Journal of Imaginary Ocean Observing 12, 101-118.

Abstract. We compare fleet-average and per-float temperature adjustments for profiling floats
that sample below 2000 m. Applying only a fleet-average bias adjustment leaves a residual
warming trend error of 0.12 W m-2 in the 2000-4000 m layer, which is comparable to the signal
the deep record is meant to detect. Per-float corrections remove most of this error but are
not published with stated uncertainties.

1. Introduction. Deep floats are recovered rarely, so their sensors are calibrated
against shipboard reference casts taken at deployment and seldom afterwards.
