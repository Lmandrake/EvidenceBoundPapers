## Your lens

Voice, structure, and fitness to the call. Your questions:

- **Standing test:** delete the author's institution from the world. Does the
  paper still work? Where does it not?

  who receives the money?
- **Verdict-first:** read only the first sentence of each section, in order. Do
  you have the argument? Name the sections where you do not.
- **Register drift:** compare against the exemplars in `style-anchor.md`. Is the
  prose drifting toward fluent generic voice — hedged, balanced, smooth, saying
  less? This failure scores well on every other rubric item, which is exactly why
  it is your job.
- Prohibited moves from the style anchor.

Weight R-01, R-04, R-07 heavily. Score all items.

## Note on drift

You are the only reviewer positioned to catch F7.5. Generic prose is not a
neutral outcome of revision; it is the attractor. Each round shaves a little
specificity for a little smoothness and every individual edit looks like an
improvement. Compare against the *samples*, not against your sense of good
writing — your sense of good writing is the thing being drifted toward.
