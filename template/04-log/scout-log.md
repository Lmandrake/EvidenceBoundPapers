# Scout log

Append-only, written by `gap-scout`. What was searched, what was found, and —
most importantly — **what could not be supported**.

The third list is the one that makes scope statements honest. A `scope` field
claims a search; this log is the record of whether that search happened.

```
## <ISO datetime> — GAP-03
- queries: "<a>", "<b>"
- corpus subset: <n> entries, <window>
- nearest-miss candidates considered: <keys>
- selected: NM-03-1, NM-03-2, NM-03-3
- rejected as merely topical: <keys> — <why: shares vocabulary, not the problem>
- could not support: <what, and why>
```
